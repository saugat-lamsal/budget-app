HOW EACH OF THE AGENT'S POINTS WAS SETTLED (Oct 10, 2026)

The agent (Phase 4's finish line, Step 2) used the app on the real server
with the made-up bank for the whole pay period, Oct 1 to Oct 16, without
seeing the code. Its instructions, the script it was given, its notes and its
report are in this folder. npm run pay-period-agent starts the same server
for it again.

Bottom line: every money number on every screen matched the rulebook on every
day, worked out by the agent by hand. What it found were things shown in words
or not shown at all, and places the rulebook does not settle. Each is settled
against the rulebook below.


THE DIFFERENCES IT FOUND

1. Money on its way between his accounts was shown nowhere (12.5).
   Rule 12.5 says money in transit is shown, and B44 (which he confirmed,
   Oct 6) says money on its way to the old card is listed but not counted.
   The screens showed only the total the check counts, in Home's check sheet.
   A bug. Tests first (views-buckets.test.ts, buckets-text.test.ts, the
   browser test buckets.test.ts, and the scripted pay period's own checks),
   then fixed: Buckets lists each transfer with one side showing, "On its
   way". QUESTIONS.md B174. Demo life: on-its-way. Breaks "[on-its-way]".

2. Payday's "Move to savings" did not add up on the screen, and said "The
   account already holds what it should" while $2,350.00 was still on its way
   (3.8). The $0.00 still to move was right: B46 already says money on its way
   to savings counts as moved. The words were not, and the money on its way
   was not shown. A bug. Tests first (views-payday.test.ts,
   views-home.test.ts, payday.test.ts), then fixed: Payday and Home's
   "Money for savings" sheet show "On its way to the account", and Payday says
   "Nothing more to move: the rest is on its way to the account." when it
   covers the rest. QUESTIONS.md B175.

3. Oct 7: the tap on the cinema charge was not refused (13.7). The agent's
   background tab read its numbers again when the tap brought it forward, and
   the tap counted against the item as it had just become, about 75 ms later.
   As built, that is what 13.7 says (the screen showed it at the moment of the
   tap). The rulebook does not say how long the screen must show something
   for it to count. Not settled: QUESTIONS.md A32, with options and a
   recommendation. The scripted pay period (Step 1) does reach the refusal.

4. The Payday summary of the pay period that ended counts the $1.50 tip from
   setup day's cafe charge in the new pay period, and shows "left in needs"
   $1,157.66 next to a question of $1,156.16. Settled by B97 (a change after
   payday to something from before it goes in the new pay period) and B105
   (what was left at payday), with B109 (the question says "less $1.50 spent
   before payday and sorted since"). No change.

5. Every likely purchase for a refund was labelled "Same name" (4.5). As built
   (B88), "made" is a name word every made-up store shares, so the label is
   true by B88 but misleading; a real statement can do the same with a city
   name. Rule 4.5 also lists fewer conditions than B88. Not settled:
   QUESTIONS.md A33, with options and a recommendation.


WHAT IT FOUND UNCLEAR IN THE RULEBOOK

- 3.8: does money on its way to savings count as moved? Settled by B46: yes.
- 10.1: what a bucket "started the pay period with" after a leftover goes to
  savings. Settled by B56: the leftover comes out of it.
- 3.4: is a purchase on payday itself "before payday"? Not written down
  before: built as no; now QUESTIONS.md B176.
- A refund on the old card. Settled by B13: it lowers the debt, no bucket.
- 4.5: can a refund match a charge that was pending on setup day? Settled by
  14.2: only the difference from what was counted on setup day moves.
- 13.5: are sorting answers logged? Settled by B41: the change log has
  settings, rules, hand moves (and take-backs); how each item was sorted is
  kept on the item.
- 13.7: the screen that read its numbers again at the tap. A32 above.
- 5.2's "debt payoff" line is called "Payment to the old card" on the screen:
  the same thing in other words.


WHAT WAS HARD TO DO ON THE SCREENS (seen, not changed)

- Typing right after a sheet opened sometimes went nowhere while it was still
  sliding in. Not a rule; the design tests check sheets once they are still.
  Worth a look on the phone.
- The date field on Setup day did not take typed digits in the agent's
  browser. On the iPhone a date field is a picker.
- At a small window, the Add rule sheet sat partly below the screen until
  scrolled. The design tests check the iPhone size (393 by 852 points).
- Each screen showed "Loading your numbers" for a few seconds: the full
  break-it run and the simulator were using every core at the time.
- Settings is long, with the change log at the bottom.
