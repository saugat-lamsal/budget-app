THE PAY PERIOD: WHAT HAPPENS AT THE BANK, AND WHAT YOU DECIDE

For 16 days, Thu Oct 1 to Fri Oct 16, 2026, you are the person the budget
app is for. Everything here is made up: the bank is a made-up bank, and no
real money is involved. The app is the one described in the rulebook
(budget-app-rulebook.md). Use it in the browser the way a person would.

How the days work
- The app shows today's date in its own world, which starts on Thu Oct 1
  (setup day). Real time does not matter.
- The control page (its address is in your instructions) has two buttons:
  "Log this browser in" (use it once, at the start, in the browser you use
  for the app) and "Bring in the next day". Each press moves the app to 7:00
  AM of the next day and puts that day's activity at the bank; the app reads
  the bank by itself right after. You can also press it from the command
  line: curl -s -X POST http://localhost:5391/next-day
- Each day: bring in the day, then do what "You decide" says, then look at
  every screen and write your notes for that day.

What the bank shows
- Amounts are what the bank shows on that account. On a card, a purchase is
  money you owe; "back" or "in" means money that came back to you.
- "Pending" is a charge the bank holds and has not posted yet. When it posts
  later, the bank shows the final amount.
- A transfer between your own accounts shows as two separate items: one
  when the money leaves an account, and one when it arrives at the other.


SETUP DAY, Thu Oct 1

Already at the bank when you start (the app has read it):
- Balances the bank shows as posted: checking $2,212.36, savings $6,150.00,
  everyday card $418.27 owed, old card $2,874.55 owed.
- Sep 30, checking: "PAYMENT FROM checking TO everyday-card", $200.00 left
  checking. It has not reached the everyday card yet (it gets there Oct 2).
- Sep 30, everyday card: MADE UP GROCER, $58.40, pending.
- Oct 1, checking (debit card): MADE UP CAFE, $6.25, pending.
- Earlier history the bank sent is just history.

You decide:
- Log in from the control page, then start setup day in the app.
- Bank rules: the ones that came with the bank connection stay as they are.
  Add one: on checking, bank text "ZELLE TO" means a payment to a person.
- The $200.00 payment to the everyday card is on its way.
- Your last paycheck before setup day came on Fri, Sep 18, 2026.
- Starting money: mom reserve $1,000.00, savings $5,150.00, wants $771.78,
  everything else in needs, nothing held for others.
- Start tracking.
- Then, on Settings, the same day: the paycheck rule is "ACME PAYROLL"; the
  mom rule is "Made Up Mom". Add three sorting rules for purchases, in this
  order: "GROCER" goes to needs, "CAFE" goes to wants, "MADE UP GAS" goes to
  needs.


Fri Oct 2

At the bank:
- Everyday card: the MADE UP GROCER charge of $58.40 posts, same amount.
- Everyday card: "SIM TRANSFER FROM checking", $200.00 arrives (the payment
  that left checking on Sep 30).
- Checking: "ACME PAYROLL DIRECT DEP", $3,200.00 in.
- Checking: MADE UP CAFE, $4.85, pending.
- That evening you moved money to savings at the bank: "PAYMENT FROM
  checking TO savings", $2,350.00 left checking (it arrives Oct 3).
You decide: nothing to answer today. Leave any payday questions for tomorrow.


Sat Oct 3

At the bank:
- Checking: the MADE UP CAFE charge from Oct 1 posts at $7.75 (it was $6.25
  pending; you added a tip).
- Checking: the MADE UP CAFE charge of $4.85 from Oct 2 posts, same amount.
- Savings: "SIM TRANSFER FROM checking", $2,350.00 arrives.
- Everyday card: MADE UP RESTAURANT, $46.20, pending.
You decide:
- The extra $1.50 on the cafe charge from setup day: needs.
- The restaurant: wants.
- Then the payday questions: keep the needs leftover where it is (roll it
  over), and move the wants leftover to savings.


Sun Oct 4

At the bank:
- Checking: MADE UP BIG BOX, $142.80, posted.
- Everyday card: MADE UP GAS, $41.10, posted.
You decide: split the big box purchase: $57.15 needs, the rest ($85.65) wants.


Mon Oct 5

At the bank:
- Everyday card: the MADE UP RESTAURANT charge posts at $55.44 (it was
  $46.20 pending; the tip).
- Everyday card: MADE UP ONLINE SHOP, $89.99, posted.
- Checking: "ZELLE TO Made Up Mom", $600.00 out.
You decide: the online shop order: needs.


Tue Oct 6

At the bank:
- Checking: MADE UP PHARMACY, $23.47, posted.
- Everyday card: MADE UP CINEMA, $18.00, pending.
You decide: the pharmacy: needs. Leave the cinema charge waiting until tomorrow.


Wed Oct 7

Before you bring in the day: open Review in the app (the cinema charge shows
$18.00, pending) and leave that tab as it is.
At the bank:
- Everyday card: the MADE UP CINEMA charge posts at $21.50.
- Checking: MADE UP GROCER, $72.15, pending.
You decide:
- Go back to the Review tab you left open, without reloading it, and sort the
  cinema charge to wants from there. Write down exactly what happens.
- If the cinema charge is still waiting after that, sort it to wants.
- Move $40.00 from wants to needs by hand.


Thu Oct 8

At the bank:
- Checking: the MADE UP GROCER charge of $72.15 posts, same amount.
- Everyday card: "MADE UP ONLINE SHOP REFUND", $30.00 back. The bank does not
  say which purchase it is for.
- Everyday card: MADE UP GAS, $41.10, posted (the gas station charged you a
  second time by mistake).
You decide: the $30.00 refund is for the $89.99 MADE UP ONLINE SHOP order of
Oct 5.


Fri Oct 9

At the bank:
- Checking: "ZELLE FROM MADE UP FRIEND", $25.00 in (a friend paying you back).
- Checking: "PAYMENT FROM checking TO old-card", $150.00 left checking (it
  reaches the old card Oct 12).
You decide:
- The friend's $25.00 is a payback, into wants.
- On Settings, change the split for your next paycheck to savings 45%, needs
  35%, wants 20%.


Sat Oct 10

At the bank:
- Everyday card: the bank takes back the second MADE UP GAS charge of $41.10
  (the copy from Oct 8).
- Checking: MADE UP ONLINE SHOP, $64.00, posted (debit card).
You decide:
- The online shop order: needs.
- On Settings, add a sorting rule for purchases: "PHARMACY" goes to needs.
  Then move it up one place.
- After you have looked at every screen and written your notes for the day,
  mark the bank's take-back as seen on Home.


Sun Oct 11

At the bank:
- Checking: MADE UP CAFE, $5.40, posted.
You decide: the MADE UP ONLINE SHOP order of Oct 5 ($89.99, $30.00 of it
refunded) was really a want: sort it again, to wants, from the list of the
bucket it is in now.


Mon Oct 12

At the bank:
- Old card: "SIM TRANSFER FROM checking", $150.00 arrives (the Oct 9 payment).
- Old card: "MADE UP STORE REFUND", $12.60 back.
- Checking: "PAYMENT FROM checking TO everyday-card", $350.00 left checking
  (it reaches the everyday card Oct 14).
You decide: nothing to answer.


Tue Oct 13

At the bank:
- Checking: "MADE UP ONLINE SHOP REFUND", $64.00 in: the store refunded the
  Oct 10 order to your debit card.
You decide: this money is the refund for the $64.00 MADE UP ONLINE SHOP order
of Oct 10.


Wed Oct 14

At the bank:
- Everyday card: "SIM TRANSFER FROM checking", $350.00 arrives.
- Everyday card: MADE UP GROCER, $96.30, posted.
You decide: nothing to answer.


Thu Oct 15

At the bank:
- Checking: MADE UP RESTAURANT, $38.00, pending.
You decide: the restaurant: wants.


Fri Oct 16

At the bank:
- Checking: "ACME PAYROLL DIRECT DEP", $3,200.00 in.
- That evening you moved money to savings at the bank: "PAYMENT FROM
  checking TO savings", $2,436.78 left checking (it is still on its way at
  the end).
You decide: the payday questions: move the needs leftover to savings, and
keep the wants leftover where it is (roll it over).

That is the end of the pay period.
