  payday"? The app did not let it lower the wants question. Also, which pay

  it. The app lowered the debt to 2,711.95 and asked nothing. That matches
  my reading of 5.4 and 5.5, but it is a reading.
- 4.5: is a charge that was pending on setup day (in the starting money,

- 13.5: "every change" is logged. The app logs settings, rules, hand moves
  and take-backs, but not sorting answers, payday answers or re-sorts. I
  read those as not "changes", but the rule does not say.
- 13.7: does a screen that refreshes itself at the moment of the tap count
  as "the screen showed it"? (Point 1 above.)
- 5.2 says a "debt payoff" line. The Needs list calls it "Payment to the
  old card". That is the same thing in other words.

  still sliding in, so I had to tap the field again. One amount field
  ended up with "1000.000.00" and had to be cleared.

  I had to set it another way.

  the screen, and its text field could not be tapped until scrolled.
- Each screen showed "Loading your numbers" for a few seconds every time.
- Nowhere could I see money on its way between accounts. The Payday
  move-to-savings figures do not add up on screen because of it.
- Settings is a very long page. The change log is at the very bottom.
- The likely-purchase labels ("Same name" on everything) make it harder to
  pick the right purchase.
