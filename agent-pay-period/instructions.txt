INSTRUCTIONS FOR THE AGENT (Phase 4's finish line, Step 2)

You are checking a personal budget app by using it the way its one user
would, for one full pay period. You have not seen its code, and you must not
look at it.

What you may read
- budget-app-rulebook.md: how the app must treat every kind of money
  movement. It is the only authority on what each number should be.
- agent-pay-period/script.txt: what happens at the bank each day, and what
  you decide.
- These instructions, and your own notes.
Do not open, read, search or list any other file in this folder or anywhere
else: no code, no tests, no other documents. Do not use the Read, Grep or
Glob tools on anything but the three files above and your own notes. If you
need to know how something works, use the app and the rulebook.

What you use
- The app, in the built-in browser (the tools named mcp__Claude_Browser__*):
  http://localhost:5390/home
- The control page: http://localhost:5391/ ("Log this browser in" once at
  the start; "Bring in the next day" each day). Or from the command line:
  curl -s -X POST http://localhost:5391/next-day
- Do not stop, restart, or change the server. Do not use any other address.
- Everything is made up: no real bank, no real money.

Each day
1. Bring in the day (the script says what the bank shows).
2. Do what "You decide" says, in the app, like a person: tap, choose, type.
   If the app asks you to confirm, say yes unless the script says otherwise.
3. Then look at every screen: Home, Buckets, the list of each bucket (Needs,
   Wants, Savings, Mom reserve, Held for others), Review, Payday, Settings.
4. For every number on every screen, write down what the rulebook says it
   should be, worked out by you from the rulebook, the script, and your own
   running record of the buckets (keep it in your notes), and what the screen
   shows. Work out the rulebook number first, then look. Show your working
   for anything that is not obvious (a split, a rounding, a cap).
5. Mark each difference clearly: DIFFERENT, with both numbers.

Your notes and report (plain text, in plain language)
- agent-pay-period/notes.txt: one section per day, written as you go (add to
  it after each day; do not wait until the end). For each screen: each
  number, "rulebook X, screen Y", and same or DIFFERENT. Also anything the
  app said in words that matters (a refusal, a warning, a question).
- agent-pay-period/report.txt, at the end: the bottom line first (did every
  number match the rulebook?), then every difference: the day, the screen,
  what the rulebook says and why (the rule number), and what the screen
  showed. Then anything in the rulebook you found unclear or that did not
  settle what a number should be, with the rule number. Then anything that
  was hard to do on the screens.
- If you are unsure whether a difference is the app's mistake or yours,
  say so, and give both workings.
