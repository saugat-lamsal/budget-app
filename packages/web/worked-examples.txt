WORKED EXAMPLES: THE SCREENS (HOME, BUCKETS, REVIEW)

Made-up numbers, worked out step by step, so you can check by hand what
the screens show. These are the demo's made-up lives (npm run demo, then
/demo). Every amount is in dollars and cents. Examples 1 to 6 are Home,
7 and 8 are Buckets, 9 to 11 are Review.

Each example is also a test. packages/web/test/worked-examples.test.ts
reads the "Check:" lines straight from this file and compares them with
what the server sends to the phone, and the browser tests
(packages/web/test/browser/worked-examples.test.ts) compare them with what
the screen shows, in Chromium and WebKit.

Settings: the starting values in rulebook section 2 (split 50/30/20, mom
reserve 1,500.00 per paycheck up to 3,000.00, low alert at 20%, paydays
every 14 days). Rules: GROCERY and GAS STATION are needs; COFFEE,
RESTAURANT, and STREAMING are wants.


========================================================================
EXAMPLE 1. A normal Wednesday (demo life "normal"; rules 3.2, 3.3, 3.4,
           4.1 to 4.3, 4.6, 8.1, 11.1, 11.2, 3.8, 9.1, 10.3, 12.3)
========================================================================

Setup day, Sat Sep 19. The last paycheck was Fri Sep 18. He assigns:
savings 8,200.00, needs 312.50, wants 184.00, mom reserve 1,200.00,
held for others 0.00.

Sep 22  GROCERY OUTLET, 64.12 on the debit card. Needs (4.1, 4.2):
        needs 312.50 - 64.12 = 248.38
Sep 26  BLUE DOOR COFFEE, 5.75 on the everyday card. Wants:
        wants 184.00 - 5.75 = 178.25

Fri Oct 2, payday: 4,200.00, 14 days after the last one, so it splits
automatically (3.7).
  Step 1. The mom reserve comes off first (3.2): 1,500.00.
          Reserve 1,200.00 + 1,500.00 = 2,700.00, under the 3,000.00 cap.
  Step 2. The rest, 4,200.00 - 1,500.00 = 2,700.00, is split (3.3):
          needs 30% = 810.00, wants 20% = 540.00,
          savings gets the rest, 2,700.00 - 810.00 - 540.00 = 1,350.00.
  Step 3. Leftover (3.4): he picks roll over for both.
          Needs 248.38 + 810.00 = 1,058.38. This is what needs started
          the pay period with (10.1).
          Wants 178.25 + 540.00 = 718.25, what wants started with.
          Savings 8,200.00 + 1,350.00 = 9,550.00.

Oct 3  ZELLE TO MADE UP MOM, 500.00. The mom rule matches (8.1):
       mom reserve 2,700.00 - 500.00 = 2,200.00
Oct 3  GROCERY OUTLET, 86.43: needs 1,058.38 - 86.43 = 971.95
Oct 4  CORNER GAS STATION, 41.20: needs 971.95 - 41.20 = 930.75
Oct 5  MAIN ST RESTAURANT, 38.90: wants 718.25 - 38.90 = 679.35
Oct 5  STREAMING SERVICE, 15.99: wants 679.35 - 15.99 = 663.36
Oct 6  ONLINE MARKETPLACE, 23.17, and CORNER STORE, 11.50. No rule
       matches either, so both wait in review (4.2, 4.3):
       unsorted spending 23.17 + 11.50 = 34.67
Oct 7  BLUE DOOR COFFEE, 6.25, still pending. It counts now (4.6):
       wants 663.36 - 6.25 = 657.11

Today, Wed Oct 7:
  Safe to spend (11.1): wants left minus unsorted spending.
          657.11 - 34.67 = 622.44
  Days to payday (11.2): Oct 2 + 14 days = Fri Oct 16, 9 days away.
  Waiting in review (10.3): the 2 purchases with no rule.
  Savings (3.8): savings 9,550.00 + mom reserve 2,200.00 = 11,750.00
          should be in the savings account. It holds 10,400.00 (this
          paycheck's 1,350.00 has not been moved yet), so
          11,750.00 - 10,400.00 = 1,350.00 is still to move.
  Neither bucket is low (10.1): needs' level is 20% of 1,058.38 = 211.676,
          and 930.75 is above it; wants' level is 20% of 718.25 = 143.65,
          and 657.11 is above it.
  The everyday card's statement, 612.48 with a 35.00 minimum, is due Wed
  Oct 14. Today is 7 days before, a reminder day (9.1).

  The self-check (12.1 to 12.3). The bank shows:
          checking 3,658.43, savings 10,400.00, everyday card owes 755.24
          (the statement's 612.48 plus every card purchase since,
          the pending coffee included:
          612.48 + 5.75 + 41.20 + 38.90 + 15.99 + 23.17 + 11.50 + 6.25).
          Bank side: 3,658.43 + 10,400.00 - 755.24 = 13,303.19
          App side: savings 9,550.00 + needs 930.75 + wants 657.11
                    + mom reserve 2,200.00 + held for others 0.00
                    - unsorted spending 34.67 = 13,303.19
          Difference: 13,303.19 - 13,303.19 = 0.00, so safe to spend shows.

Check: safe to spend 622.44 | needs 930.75 | wants 657.11 | held back 34.67
Check: still to move 1,350.00 | difference 0.00 | days to payday 9 | waiting 2
Check: needs started with 1,058.38 | wants started with 718.25

EXAMPLE 2. Totals that do not match the bank (demo life "mismatch";
           rules 0.5, 12.3, B37)
========================================================================

The same life as example 1, but the bank shows checking at 3,646.09:
12.34 less than in example 1.

  Bank side: 3,646.09 + 10,400.00 - 755.24 = 13,290.85

EXAMPLE 5. The bank takes back a charge it showed twice (demo life
           "took-back"; rules 4.10, 10.6, QUESTIONS.md B84)
========================================================================

The same life as example 1, but on Oct 5 the bank shows MAIN ST
RESTAURANT, 38.90, twice. On Oct 6 it takes the copy back.

Oct 5  Both charges count, wants by his rule:
       wants 718.25 - 38.90 - 38.90 = 640.45
       then STREAMING SERVICE, 15.99: 640.45 - 15.99 = 624.46
Oct 6  The bank takes the copy back. The app takes it back too (4.10): its
       38.90 goes back to wants. 624.46 + 38.90 = 663.36
Oct 7  The pending coffee, 6.25: 663.36 - 6.25 = 657.11, as in example 1.

  Safe to spend: 657.11 - 34.67 = 622.44, as in example 1.
  Needs attention shows "The bank took back a transaction: MAIN ST
  RESTAURANT, $38.90, on Tue, Oct 6." It stays there until he taps it and
  says he has seen it (B84).

Check: wants 657.11 | safe to spend 622.44 | taken back 38.90

EXAMPLE 7. Buckets on a normal Wednesday (demo life "normal"; rules
           12.2, 10.1, 8.2, 7.3, 12.4, 4.3)
========================================================================

The same life as example 1. Buckets shows what is in each bucket:

  Savings: 9,550.00 (example 1)
  Needs 930.75 and wants 657.11 (example 1)
  Mom reserve: 2,200.00 (example 1)
  Held for others: 0.00, settled (7.3)
  Waiting in review, not in any bucket yet: the two purchases with no
          rule, 34.67 going out (4.3)
  The one main number is everything in the buckets (12.2): savings, needs,
          wants, mom reserve, held for others, and unsorted money in,
          less unsorted spending:
          9,550.00 + 930.75 + 657.11 + 2,200.00 + 0.00 - 34.67
          = 13,303.19, the same app side as example 1.

  Mom this month (8.2): October so far he sent 500.00 (Oct 3). The target
  is 2,000.00 to 3,000.00 a month. 500.00 is under 2,000.00, so Buckets
  says "Under target".

  The old card (12.4): 2,980.00 owed. It is not part of the total.

Check: total 13,303.19 | savings 9,550.00 | needs 930.75 | wants 657.11
Check: mom reserve 2,200.00 | mom sent 500.00 | held for others 0.00
Check: old card 2,980.00 | waiting out 34.67

EXAMPLE 9. A paycheck-looking deposit that came early asks first (demo
           life "review-busy"; rules 3.7, 6.1, 3.2, 3.3, B1, B52)
========================================================================

The same life as example 1, but with 17 things waiting in review (the
two payday questions, a deposit, an early paycheck, a refund, the two
purchases from example 1 and more).

Oct 6  MADE UP EMPLOYER PAYROLL BONUS, 300.00. It matches the paycheck
       rule, but it came 4 days after the Oct 2 paycheck, less than 10, so
       it waits in review instead of splitting by itself (3.7).

He taps "This is my paycheck". That cannot be undone here, so the screen
asks first, and shows what it would do (3.2, 3.3):
  The mom reserve comes off first: the most it takes is 1,500.00, but the
          deposit is only 300.00, so it takes 300.00 (B1). The reserve
          holds 0.00 by then (a 2,500.00 payment to mom took the 2,200.00
          it had), and it has room up to 3,000.00, so all 300.00 fits.
  Nothing is left to split: 300.00 - 300.00 = 0.00, so needs 0.00, wants
          0.00, and savings 0.00.
  The parts add up to the deposit: 300.00 + 0.00 + 0.00 + 0.00 = 300.00

Only after he says "Yes, this is my paycheck" is anything sent.

Check: preview mom 300.00 | preview savings 0.00 | preview needs 0.00
Check: preview wants 0.00 | waiting 17


========================================================================
EXAMPLE 10. One purchase split, the rest worked out for him (demo life
            "normal"; rules 4.4, 4.7, 12.2)
========================================================================

The same life as example 1. CORNER STORE, 11.50, has no rule, so it waits
in review (4.2). He splits it: 4.00 to needs. Wants gets the rest. The
screen does not work out the rest; the money math does:
  The rest: 11.50 - 4.00 = 7.50
  Needs: 930.75 - 4.00 = 926.75
  Wants: 657.11 - 7.50 = 649.61
  Unsorted spending: 34.67 - 11.50 = 23.17 (only ONLINE MARKETPLACE is
          left waiting)
  Everything in the buckets did not change (12.2): sorting only moves
          money from "unsorted" to the buckets:
          9,550.00 + 926.75 + 649.61 + 2,200.00 + 0.00 - 23.17
          = 13,303.19

Check: split needs 4.00 | split wants 7.50 | needs after 926.75
Check: wants after 649.61 | waiting out after 23.17 | total after 13,303.19

EXAMPLE 11. A refund lists the purchases it likely belongs to (demo life
            "review-busy"; rules 4.5, 20d, QUESTIONS.md A15)
========================================================================

The same life as example 9. On Oct 7 the everyday card shows a refund of
23.17, ONLINE MARKETPLACE REFUND. The bank did not say which purchase it
was for, so it waits in review (4.5). The app lists the purchases it
likely belongs to: on the same card, on or before Oct 7, with at least
23.17 left to refund, and either the same name or the same amount.
  ONLINE MARKETPLACE, 23.17, Oct 6: the same name and the same amount.
          It comes first.
  ONLINE MARKETPLACE, 54.00, Oct 1: the same name, and 54.00 is at
          least 23.17. It comes second.
  MAIN ST RESTAURANT, 38.90 has enough left, but neither its name nor
          its amount is the refund's, so it is not listed.
Two purchases are listed. Putting the refund back in a bucket is his
choice, one tap (payback, 6.1).

Check: likely count 2 | likely first 23.17 | likely second 54.00
