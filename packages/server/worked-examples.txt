WORKED EXAMPLES: READING THE BANK

Made-up numbers, worked out step by step, so you can check by hand how the
app reads what the bank sends. Every amount is in dollars and cents.

Each example is also a test (packages/server/test/worked-examples.test.ts).
The test reads the "Check:" lines straight from this file and compares them
with what the app works out from the bank's own answers.

How the bank connection (Plaid) writes amounts: a positive amount is money
that left the account, a negative one is money that came in, on every kind
of account. A card's balance is what you owe on it. The posted balance does
not include anything pending.


========================================================================
EXAMPLE 1. A week at the test bank (rules 14.2, 12.1 to 12.5, 4.6, A2,
           A10, 5.1 to 5.3, 3.2, 3.3)
========================================================================

These are the saved bank answers in
packages/server/test/fixtures/a-week-at-the-test-bank.json.

Setup day, Oct 1. What the bank shows:

Checking, posted balance                         2,450.75
  pending: GAS STATION 77                           46.23
Savings, posted balance                          6,100.00
Everyday card, owed                                412.30
  pending: COFFEE SHOP 5                            12.00
Old card, owed                                   2,980.44   (not in the self-check, 12.4)

Step 1. The starting money (14.2): checking + savings - everyday card,
        with pending charges included.
        Checking with pending        2,450.75 - 46.23 = 2,404.52
        Everyday card with pending     412.30 + 12.00 =   424.30
        2,404.52 + 6,100.00 - 424.30 = 8,080.22

Step 2. He assigns it: savings 5,000.00, mom reserve 1,100.00, wants
        300.00, held for others 0.00, and needs the rest:
        8,080.22 - 5,000.00 - 1,100.00 - 300.00 = 1,680.22
        The two pending charges are already in the starting money, so they
        take nothing from a bucket (A10).

The next sync brings the week, Oct 2 to Oct 9:

Oct 2  The gas charge posts at 48.10. The bank shows this as the pending
       charge removed and a posted one added, linked to it. It was counted
       at 46.23 on setup day, so only the difference, 1.87, waits in review
       (4.6, A10).
       The coffee hold is removed with nothing posted in its place: it was
       dropped (A2). It was counted at 12.00 on setup day, so -12.00 waits
       in review, to go back into a bucket (A10).
Oct 3  300.00 from checking to savings: both halves seen. No bucket
       changes (5.3).
Oct 4  412.30 leaves checking for the everyday card. It is in transit.
Oct 5  It lands on the card. Nothing in transit. No bucket changes (5.1).
Oct 6  COFFEE SHOP 5 for 18.75 on the everyday card, wants by his rule:
       wants 300.00 - 18.75 = 281.25
Oct 6  150.00 leaves checking for the old card, out of needs (5.2):
       needs 1,680.22 - 150.00 = 1,530.22
Oct 7  It lands on the old card:
       old card debt 2,980.44 - 150.00 = 2,830.44
Oct 8  Interest of 7.89 on the everyday card waits in review (4.8).
Oct 9  Paycheck 2,345.67, 14 days after the last one (Sep 25).
       Mom reserve first: 1,100.00 + 1,500.00 = 2,600.00, under the cap.
       Rest to split: 2,345.67 - 1,500.00 = 845.67
       Savings 50% = 422.835 -> 422.83, needs 30% = 253.701 -> 253.70,
       wants 20% = 169.134 -> 169.13. The 0.01 left over goes to savings:
       422.84 (3.3).
       savings 5,000.00 + 422.84 = 5,422.84
       needs   1,530.22 + 253.70 = 1,783.92
       wants     281.25 + 169.13 =   450.38

Waiting in review as spending: 1.87 - 12.00 + 7.89 = -2.24

What the bank shows after the week (nothing pending now):
Checking   2,450.75 - 48.10 - 300.00 - 412.30 - 150.00 + 2,345.67 = 3,886.02
Savings    6,100.00 + 300.00 = 6,400.00
Everyday card owed   412.30 - 412.30 + 18.75 + 7.89 = 26.64
Bank side (12.1): 3,886.02 + 6,400.00 - 26.64 = 10,259.38

App side (12.2): savings + needs + wants + mom reserve + held for others
                 + unsorted money in - unsorted spending
   5,422.84 + 1,783.92 + 450.38 + 2,600.00 + 0.00 + 0.00 - (-2.24) = 10,259.38
Difference (12.3): 10,259.38 + 0.00 in transit - 10,259.38 = 0.00

Check: starting money 8,080.22 | savings 5,422.84 | needs 1,783.92 | wants 450.38 | mom reserve 2,600.00
Check: unsorted spending -2.24 | bank side 10,259.38 | difference 0.00 | old card debt 2,830.44


========================================================================
EXAMPLE 2. Pending money in waits until it posts (rule 12.1, B36)
========================================================================

Checking, posted balance                         1,000.00
  pending: a charge, GROCERY                        30.00
  pending: a deposit coming in                     200.00
Savings                                              0.00
Everyday card, owed                                  0.00

Plaid's "available" balance takes off pending money out and adds pending
money in: 1,000.00 - 30.00 + 200.00 = 1,170.00. The app does not use it.

Bank side (12.1): pending charges count, pending money in does not until it
posts: 1,000.00 - 30.00 + 0.00 - 0.00 = 970.00

Check: bank side 970.00 | available 1,170.00


========================================================================
EXAMPLE 3. The bank changes its mind (rules 4.6, 4.9, 4.10, 12.1; A13,
           A14, A16)
========================================================================

Setup day, Oct 1: checking 3,000.00, savings 5,000.00, everyday card owes
450.00, nothing pending. Starting money 3,000.00 + 5,000.00 - 450.00 =
7,550.00. He assigns savings 4,000.00, mom reserve 1,000.00, wants 200.00,
and needs the rest, 2,350.00. A sorting rule sends "COFFEE" to wants.

Step 1. Oct 2: a coffee bar tab, 20.00, shows pending on the everyday card.
        Wants 200.00 - 20.00 = 180.00

Step 2. Oct 3: still pending, the bank changes the tab to 26.00 (a tip).
        The new amount counts right away (A13):
        Wants 180.00 - 6.00 = 174.00
        The same day a 10.00 coffee posts on checking, and the bank shows
        it twice:
        Wants 174.00 - 10.00 - 10.00 = 154.00

Step 3. Oct 4: the bank takes the copy back. The app takes it back too
        (4.10), logs it, and tells him:
        Wants 154.00 + 10.00 = 164.00
        A 75.00 bill is paid straight from savings. Nothing names it, so
        it waits in review to be sorted (4.9):
        Unsorted spending 75.00

Where the bank stands (12.1):
        Checking       3,000.00 - 10.00 = 2,990.00
        Savings        5,000.00 - 75.00 = 4,925.00
        Everyday card  450.00 + 26.00 pending = 476.00
        Bank side      2,990.00 + 4,925.00 - 476.00 = 7,439.00
Where the app stands (12.2):
        4,000.00 + 2,350.00 + 164.00 + 1,000.00 - 75.00 = 7,439.00
        The difference is 0.00.

Check: wants 164.00 | unsorted spending 75.00 | bank side 7,439.00 | difference 0.00 | taken back 10.00
