WORKED EXAMPLES: READING THE BANK

Made-up numbers, worked out step by step, so you can check by hand how the
app reads what the bank sends. Every amount is in dollars and cents.

Each example is also a test (packages/server/test/worked-examples.test.ts).
The test reads the "Check:" lines straight from this file and compares them
with what the app works out from the bank's own answers.

How the bank connection (Plaid) writes amounts: a positive amount is money
that left the account, a negative one is money that came in, on every kind
of account. A card's balance is what you owe on it. The posted balance does
not include anything pending.

EXAMPLE 1. A week at the test bank (rules 14.2, 12.1 to 12.5, 4.6, A2,
           A10, 5.1 to 5.3, 3.2, 3.3)
========================================================================

These are the saved bank answers in
packages/server/test/fixtures/a-week-at-the-test-bank.json.

Setup day, Oct 1. What the bank shows:

Checking, posted balance                         2,450.75
  pending: GAS STATION 77                           46.23
Savings, posted balance                          6,100.00
Everyday card, owed                                412.30
  pending: COFFEE SHOP 5                            12.00
Old card, owed                                   2,980.44   (not in the self-check, 12.4)

Step 1. The starting money (14.2): checking + savings - everyday card,
        with pending charges included.
        Checking with pending        2,450.75 - 46.23 = 2,404.52
        Everyday card with pending     412.30 + 12.00 =   424.30
        2,404.52 + 6,100.00 - 424.30 = 8,080.22

Step 2. He assigns it: savings 5,000.00, mom reserve 1,100.00, wants
        300.00, held for others 0.00, and needs the rest:
        8,080.22 - 5,000.00 - 1,100.00 - 300.00 = 1,680.22
        The two pending charges are already in the starting money, so they
        take nothing from a bucket (A10).

The next sync brings the week, Oct 2 to Oct 9:

Oct 2  The gas charge posts at 48.10. The bank shows this as the pending
       charge removed and a posted one added, linked to it. It was counted
       at 46.23 on setup day, so only the difference, 1.87, waits in review
       (4.6, A10).
       The coffee hold is removed with nothing posted in its place: it was
       dropped (A2). It was counted at 12.00 on setup day, so -12.00 waits
       in review, to go back into a bucket (A10).
Oct 3  300.00 from checking to savings: both halves seen. No bucket
       changes (5.3).
Oct 4  412.30 leaves checking for the everyday card. It is in transit.
Oct 5  It lands on the card. Nothing in transit. No bucket changes (5.1).
Oct 6  COFFEE SHOP 5 for 18.75 on the everyday card, wants by his rule:
       wants 300.00 - 18.75 = 281.25
Oct 6  150.00 leaves checking for the old card, out of needs (5.2):
       needs 1,680.22 - 150.00 = 1,530.22
Oct 7  It lands on the old card:
       old card debt 2,980.44 - 150.00 = 2,830.44
Oct 8  Interest of 7.89 on the everyday card waits in review (4.8).
Oct 9  Paycheck 2,345.67, 14 days after the last one (Sep 25).
       Mom reserve first: 1,100.00 + 1,500.00 = 2,600.00, under the cap.
       Rest to split: 2,345.67 - 1,500.00 = 845.67
       Savings 50% = 422.835 -> 422.83, needs 30% = 253.701 -> 253.70,
       wants 20% = 169.134 -> 169.13. The 0.01 left over goes to savings:
       422.84 (3.3).
       savings 5,000.00 + 422.84 = 5,422.84
       needs   1,530.22 + 253.70 = 1,783.92
       wants     281.25 + 169.13 =   450.38

Waiting in review as spending: 1.87 - 12.00 + 7.89 = -2.24

What the bank shows after the week (nothing pending now):
Checking   2,450.75 - 48.10 - 300.00 - 412.30 - 150.00 + 2,345.67 = 3,886.02
Savings    6,100.00 + 300.00 = 6,400.00
Everyday card owed   412.30 - 412.30 + 18.75 + 7.89 = 26.64
Bank side (12.1): 3,886.02 + 6,400.00 - 26.64 = 10,259.38

App side (12.2): savings + needs + wants + mom reserve + held for others
                 + unsorted money in - unsorted spending
   5,422.84 + 1,783.92 + 450.38 + 2,600.00 + 0.00 + 0.00 - (-2.24) = 10,259.38
Difference (12.3): 10,259.38 + 0.00 in transit - 10,259.38 = 0.00

Check: starting money 8,080.22 | savings 5,422.84 | needs 1,783.92 | wants 450.38 | mom reserve 2,600.00
Check: unsorted spending -2.24 | bank side 10,259.38 | difference 0.00 | old card debt 2,830.44

EXAMPLE 3. The bank changes its mind (rules 4.6, 4.9, 4.10, 12.1; A13,
           A14, A16)
========================================================================

Setup day, Oct 1: checking 3,000.00, savings 5,000.00, everyday card owes
450.00, nothing pending. Starting money 3,000.00 + 5,000.00 - 450.00 =
7,550.00. He assigns savings 4,000.00, mom reserve 1,000.00, wants 200.00,
and needs the rest, 2,350.00. A sorting rule sends "COFFEE" to wants.

Step 1. Oct 2: a coffee bar tab, 20.00, shows pending on the everyday card.
        Wants 200.00 - 20.00 = 180.00

Step 2. Oct 3: still pending, the bank changes the tab to 26.00 (a tip).
        The new amount counts right away (A13):
        Wants 180.00 - 6.00 = 174.00
        The same day a 10.00 coffee posts on checking, and the bank shows
        it twice:
        Wants 174.00 - 10.00 - 10.00 = 154.00

Step 3. Oct 4: the bank takes the copy back. The app takes it back too
        (4.10), logs it, and tells him:
        Wants 154.00 + 10.00 = 164.00
        A 75.00 bill is paid straight from savings. Nothing names it, so
        it waits in review to be sorted (4.9):
        Unsorted spending 75.00

Where the bank stands (12.1):
        Checking       3,000.00 - 10.00 = 2,990.00
        Savings        5,000.00 - 75.00 = 4,925.00
        Everyday card  450.00 + 26.00 pending = 476.00
        Bank side      2,990.00 + 4,925.00 - 476.00 = 7,439.00
Where the app stands (12.2):
        4,000.00 + 2,350.00 + 164.00 + 1,000.00 - 75.00 = 7,439.00
        The difference is 0.00.

Check: wants 164.00 | unsorted spending 75.00 | bank side 7,439.00 | difference 0.00 | taken back 10.00

EXAMPLE 4. A bank rule added after setup day reads what comes next; what was already read stays (section 2, 24c, 4.2, 4.3, 7.4, 12.1 to 12.3)
========================================================================

The same start as example 3: checking 3,000.00, savings 5,000.00, the
everyday card owes 450.00. Starting money 3,000.00 + 5,000.00 - 450.00 =

2,350.00. No bank rule names "MADE UP GYM", and no sorting rule does.

Step 1. Oct 2: 40.00 to MADE UP GYM leaves checking. No bank rule names it,

        Unsorted spending 40.00

Step 3. Oct 4: another 40.00 to MADE UP GYM leaves checking. The new rule
        reads it as a payment to a person, which waits in review too (7.4):

Where the bank stands (12.1):
        Checking       3,000.00 - 40.00 - 40.00 = 2,920.00

Where the app stands (12.2):
        4,000.00 + 2,350.00 + 200.00 + 1,000.00 - 80.00 = 7,470.00
        The difference is 0.00. No cent was made or lost by the change.

Check: first gym payment 40.00 | second gym payment 40.00 | unsorted spending 80.00 | bank side 7,470.00 | difference 0.00

EXAMPLE 5. Moving a bank rule up: the first rule that fits wins (section 2, A27, 5.2, 7.4, 12.1 to 12.5)
========================================================================

The same start as example 3: checking 3,000.00, savings 5,000.00, the
everyday card owes 450.00. Starting money 3,000.00 + 5,000.00 - 450.00 =
7,550.00: savings 4,000.00, mom reserve 1,000.00, wants 200.00, needs
2,350.00.

Step 1. Oct 2: he adds two bank rules on Settings, in this order. Each new
        rule goes to the end of the list:
        (a) "Online Banking transfer" on checking: a payment to a person
            (he pays his landlord that way);
        (b) "Online Banking transfer to CRD 4444" on checking: a transfer
            between checking and the old card.
        A payment to the old card fits both. The first rule that fits wins
        (A27), and (a) comes first.

Step 2. Oct 3: 100.00 "Online Banking transfer to CRD 4444" leaves
        checking. Rule (a) reads it as a payment to a person, so it waits
        in review (7.4):
        Unsorted spending 100.00

Step 3. Oct 3: he moves rule (b) up. Only rules on checking compete for
        what leaves checking, so it moves past (a), the rule before it there.
        Before he says yes, Settings shows what would change hands: 1 item
        from the last 90 days, the Oct 3 payment, that (a) reads now. The move
        reads what the bank sends from then on. The Oct 3 payment was read
        already, so it stays waiting in review (he can mark it as a transfer
        there, 5.6).

Step 4. Oct 4: another 100.00 "Online Banking transfer to CRD 4444" leaves
        checking. Now rule (b) reads it first: a payment to the old card from
        checking, which comes out of needs on a "debt payoff" line (5.2):
        Needs 2,350.00 - 100.00 = 2,250.00
        It is on its way to the old card: shown, but not counted, because the
        old card is not part of the check (12.4, 12.5).

Where the bank stands (12.1):
        Checking       3,000.00 - 100.00 - 100.00 = 2,800.00
        Bank side      2,800.00 + 5,000.00 - 450.00 = 7,350.00
Where the app stands (12.2):
        4,000.00 + 2,250.00 + 200.00 + 1,000.00 - 100.00 = 7,350.00
        The difference is 0.00. Moving the rule made or lost no cent.

Check: unsorted spending 100.00 | second payment 100.00 | needs 2,250.00 | bank side 7,350.00 | difference 0.00

EXAMPLE 6. A restart in the middle of a pay period finds the books as they were (the real server keeps everything; 4.2, 4.3, 13.3, 13.5)
========================================================================

Setup day, Oct 1, the same start as example 3: checking 3,000.00, savings
5,000.00, the everyday card owes 450.00. Starting money 7,550.00: savings
4,000.00, mom reserve 1,000.00, wants 200.00, needs 2,350.00.

The history keeps every event in order, each with the day it was made on
and the totals after it:

Event   Day     What                                         Wants   Needs  Unsorted
setup   Oct 1   setup day                                   200.00  2,350.00    0.00
bank    Oct 3   23.45 at CORNER STORE 12, on the everyday
                card; no rule matches, so it waits (4.3)    200.00  2,350.00   23.45
answer  Oct 3   he puts it in wants (4.2)                   176.55  2,350.00    0.00
answer  Oct 3   he moves 50.00 from wants to needs (13.3)   126.55  2,400.00    0.00

        200.00 - 23.45 = 176.55;  176.55 - 50.00 = 126.55;  2,350.00 + 50.00 = 2,400.00

The server stops and starts again. Before it serves anything, it replays
the history one event at a time and compares each event's totals with the
ones saved: they are the same, so it starts, with wants 126.55 and needs
2,400.00, and the change log still says "Moved from Wants to Needs, 50.00,
Oct 3".

His phone, which never got the answer to the move, sends it again with the
same decision number. The answer saved with it comes back, and nothing
moves a second time: wants stays 126.55.

Had the code been changed so that the purchase went to needs instead, the
replay would give wants 200.00 after his first answer, not the 176.55
saved: the server would not start, and would say it was that answer, on
Oct 3.

Check: wants 126.55 | needs 2,400.00 | unsorted spending 0.00 | savings 4,000.00 | mom reserve 1,000.00 | moved 50.00

EXAMPLE 7. Today is the date in his home time zone (section 2)
========================================================================

His home time zone is America/New_York, as it starts. The server's own
clock may be set to any zone; it does not matter.

At 3:30 AM on Oct 3 in UTC it is 11:30 PM on Oct 2 in New York (New York
is 4 hours behind UTC until Nov 1).
  - Today is Oct 2.
  - A purchase of 15.00 the bank dates Oct 3 keeps the bank's date, Oct 3.
  - A hand move of 1.00 made then is dated Oct 2, in the history and in the
    change log.

At 5:30 AM on Oct 10 in UTC it is 1:30 AM on Oct 10 in New York, so today
is Oct 10. He changes his home time zone to America/Los_Angeles, 7 hours
behind UTC: there it is 10:30 PM on Oct 9, so today is Oct 9 from then on.
The change itself is dated Oct 10, the day it was made.

Dates: now 2026-10-02 | bank purchase 2026-10-03 | hand move 2026-10-02 | after the change 2026-10-09 | change made 2026-10-10
Check: purchase 15.00 | moved 1.00

EXAMPLE 9. Syncing by itself, "Checked at", and an answer about something the sync changed (section 2, 4.6, 0.5; Step 3; QUESTIONS.md A31)
========================================================================

The server syncs with the bank every 60 minutes of real time, counted from
when the last sync started. Setup day was Oct 1: Wants 200.00. Home shows
"Checked at" with the time the bank was last read, in New York time.

Sat Oct 31, 11:30 PM in New York (EDT, 4 hours behind UTC): the server
starts. The bank has never been read since it was set up, so it syncs at
once. MADE UP CAFE 11.50 shows on the everyday card, pending, and waits in
review. He opens Review: it shows the cafe at 11.50, pending.

Clocks in New York go back an hour at 2:00 AM on Nov 1 (EDT to EST, 5 hours
behind UTC). The syncs come 60 minutes apart all the same:
  Oct 31 11:30 PM EDT   Checked at 11:30 PM (the first)
  Nov 1  12:30 AM EDT   the cafe has posted at 13.50, with a tip (4.6)
          1:30 AM EDT
          1:30 AM EST   one hour later, though the clock says 1:30 again
          2:30 AM EST

Then he taps Wants on the cafe, on the Review he opened at 11:30 PM. It
showed 11.50, pending, so the answer is refused: "MADE UP CAFE changed
since you looked: it is now $13.50. Look at it again, then choose. Nothing
was changed." Wants is still 200.00, and nothing is added to the history.
He looks again and taps Wants: 200.00 - 13.50 = 186.50.

At 3:30 AM EST the bank cannot be reached: that sync saves nothing, and
Home still says Checked at 2:30 AM. The next sync is one interval later,
at 4:30 AM EST, and reads the bank.

Check: cafe pending 11.50 | cafe posted 13.50 | wants after 186.50
Checked at: 11:30 PM EDT | 12:30 AM EDT | 1:30 AM EDT | 1:30 AM EST | 2:30 AM EST | 2:30 AM EST | 4:30 AM EST

EXAMPLE 14. Back after three evenings down (10.9, 10.7; QUESTIONS.md A36, A38)
========================================================================

When the server comes back, of what came due while it was down it sends
only the newest of each kind, and only if it is still true: one daily
review, one reminder per card. Each says when it was due. Setup day was
Oct 1. The everyday card's statement closed Oct 3: balance 450.00,
minimum 35.00, due Sat Oct 10. Reminders come 7 and 2 days before. All
times are New York time (EDT).

Sat Oct 3, noon. The server reads the bank, with the statement.
6:00 PM. It reads the bank again: MADE UP SHOP 12.00 waits in review.
That is the last good read.
7:30 PM. The server stops. It is down over the evenings of Sat Oct 3,
Sun Oct 4 and Mon Oct 5.

What came due while it was down:
  Sat Oct 3, 8:00 PM   the daily review (1 thing waits), and the everyday
                       card's reminder: Oct 10 is 7 days away, nothing paid
  Sun Oct 4, 6:00 PM   the bank not read for 24 hours (10.7)
  Sun Oct 4, 8:00 PM   the daily review
  Mon Oct 5, 8:00 PM   the daily review

Tue Oct 6, 7:30 AM. The server starts again. Of the three daily reviews,
only the newest, Oct 5's. 1 thing still waits, so it is still true:
"Time to review: 1 thing is waiting for you to sort." It says "Due Mon,

Of the card, its newest reminder, Oct 3's. Oct 10 is now 4 days away:

Times: review due 8:00 PM EDT | review sent 7:30 AM EDT | reminder due 8:00 PM EDT | reminder sent 7:30 AM EDT | not read due 6:00 PM EDT | not read sent 7:30 AM EDT
Check: shop 12.00 | statement balance 450.00 | minimum 35.00 | alerts sent 3.00
