WORKED EXAMPLES

Made-up numbers, worked out step by step, so you can check the money math
by hand. Every amount is in dollars and cents.

Each example is also a test (packages/money/test/worked-examples.test.ts).
The test reads the "Check:" line of every example straight from this file
and compares it with what the app works out. If a number here is wrong, or
the app disagrees with it, the test fails.

Unless an example says otherwise, the settings are the starting ones from
the rulebook: split 50% savings / 30% needs / 20% wants, mom reserve
$1,500.00 a paycheck, capped at $3,000.00.

Rounding: each share is rounded DOWN to the cent. Any cents left over from
rounding go to savings (rule 3.3).

EXAMPLE 1. A normal paycheck (rules 3.2, 3.3)
========================================================================

Before payday: every bucket is at $0.00.

Paycheck                                         2,345.67

Step 1. The mom reserve comes off first.
        Reserve is $0.00, cap is $3,000.00, so there is room.
        To mom reserve                          -1,500.00
        Rest to split                              845.67

Step 2. Split the rest.
        Savings 50% of 845.67 = 422.835  ->  422.83
        Needs   30% of 845.67 = 253.701  ->  253.70
        Wants   20% of 845.67 = 169.134  ->  169.13
        Rounded shares add to 845.66, so 0.01 is left over.
        The leftover 0.01 goes to savings: 422.83 + 0.01 = 422.84

Step 3. Add it back up: 1,500.00 + 422.84 + 253.70 + 169.13 = 2,345.67
        That is the paycheck exactly.

Check: mom reserve 1,500.00 | savings 422.84 | needs 253.70 | wants 169.13

EXAMPLE 2. The mom reserve is close to its cap (rule 3.2)
========================================================================

        Savings 50% of 1,500.00 =   750.00
        Needs   30% of 1,500.00 =   450.00
        Wants   20% of 1,500.00 =   300.00
        No rounding needed.

Step 3. Buckets after payday.
        Mom reserve  2,600.00 + 400.00            = 3,000.00 (at the cap)
        Savings      1,100.00 + 750.00            = 1,850.00
        Needs                                         450.00
        Wants                                         300.00
        Paycheck check: 400.00 + 1,850.00 + 450.00 + 300.00 = 3,000.00

Check: mom reserve 3,000.00 | savings 1,850.00 | needs 450.00 | wants 300.00

           (rules 3.2, 3.3, 3.4, 3.5, 3.8)

EXAMPLE 17. The old card payoff countdown (rule 9.5)
========================================================================

Before: The old card owes 3,000.00. The last paycheck came Sep 25, and pay
        comes every 14 days: Oct 9, Oct 23, Nov 6, Nov 20, Dec 4, Dec 18,
        Jan 1, Jan 15, and so on. Today is Oct 6. The promo end dates are

        Oct 9 to Jan 1, 7 paydays.
        3,000.00 / 7 = 428.5714..., rounded up to 428.58 per payday.

        3,000.00 - 6 x 428.58 = 3,000.00 - 2,571.48 = 428.52

        does not count: Oct 9 to Dec 18, 6 paydays.

        3,000.00 is due now.

Before: Wants 200.00. A rule sends "MADE UP CINEMA" to wants.

        Wants 137.00 + 31.50 = 168.50

Check: wants 168.50 | unsorted spending 0.00 | taken back 31.50

EXAMPLE 23. A payment for mom, and one split three ways (rules 7.6, 8.2, 8.3, 7.3; B20)
========================================================================

Before: Needs 400.00, wants 150.00, mom reserve 250.00, held for others
        0.00.

Step 1. You send 300.00 to your aunt, for mom. No rule knows your aunt, so
        it waits in review, and you sort it "for mom". It comes out of the
        mom reserve as far as the reserve goes, and counts toward this
        month's money to mom (8.2):
        From the reserve: 250.00 of the 300.00
        Mom reserve 250.00 - 250.00 = 0.00
        Sent to mom this month: 300.00
        The other 50.00 waits for you to pick a bucket (8.3).

Step 2. You put the 50.00 in needs:
        Needs 400.00 - 50.00 = 350.00

Step 3. You pay a friend 90.00 for a dinner and groceries. You split it:
        30.00 was your dinner (wants), 15.00 your share of the groceries
        (needs), and 45.00 your roommate owes you back (not mine, 7.6):
        Wants 150.00 - 30.00 = 120.00
        Needs 350.00 - 15.00 = 335.00
        Held for others 0.00 - 45.00 = -45.00, which means someone owes
        you 45.00 (7.3).

Check: needs 335.00 | wants 120.00 | mom reserve 0.00 | held for others -45.00 | sent to mom 300.00 | unsorted spending 0.00

EXAMPLE 24. A purchase from before payday that shows up after it (rules 3.4, 4.1; B4)
========================================================================

Before: Savings 1,000.00, needs 300.00, wants 120.00, mom reserve
        3,000.00 (at its cap). Needs rolls over at payday; for wants the
        app asks. A rule sends "MADE UP CAFE" to wants.

Step 1. Friday Oct 9 is payday: 2,000.00. The reserve is at its cap, so
        its 1,500.00 goes to savings (3.2). The other 500.00 is split:
        Needs 30% = 150.00, wants 20% = 100.00, savings 50% = 250.00
        Savings 1,000.00 + 1,500.00 + 250.00 = 2,750.00
        Needs 300.00 + 150.00 = 450.00
        Wants 120.00 + 100.00 = 220.00
        The question: wants had 120.00 left at payday. Roll it over, or
        move it to savings?

Step 2. On Saturday, a 45.00 cafe charge from Thursday, before payday,
        shows up. It comes out of wants on the day it was made (4.1), so
        the question was asking about too much. It now asks about
        120.00 - 45.00 = 75.00 (never below zero):
        Wants 220.00 - 45.00 = 175.00

Step 3. You answer "move to savings". 75.00 moves:
        Wants 175.00 - 75.00 = 100.00
        Savings 2,750.00 + 75.00 = 2,825.00

Check: savings 2,825.00 | needs 450.00 | wants 100.00 | moved to savings 75.00
