WORKED EXAMPLES

Made-up numbers, worked out step by step, so you can check the money math
by hand. Every amount is in dollars and cents.

Each example is also a test (packages/money/test/worked-examples.test.ts).
The test reads the "Check:" line of every example straight from this file
and compares it with what the app works out. If a number here is wrong, or
the app disagrees with it, the test fails.

Unless an example says otherwise, the settings are the starting ones from
the rulebook: split 50% savings / 30% needs / 20% wants, mom reserve
$1,500.00 a paycheck, capped at $3,000.00.

Rounding: each share is rounded DOWN to the cent. Any cents left over from
rounding go to savings (rule 3.3).


========================================================================
EXAMPLE 1. A normal paycheck (rules 3.2, 3.3)
========================================================================

Before payday: every bucket is at $0.00.

Paycheck                                         2,345.67

Step 1. The mom reserve comes off first.
        Reserve is $0.00, cap is $3,000.00, so there is room.
        To mom reserve                          -1,500.00
        Rest to split                              845.67

Step 2. Split the rest.
        Savings 50% of 845.67 = 422.835  ->  422.83
        Needs   30% of 845.67 = 253.701  ->  253.70
        Wants   20% of 845.67 = 169.134  ->  169.13
        Rounded shares add to 845.66, so 0.01 is left over.
        The leftover 0.01 goes to savings: 422.83 + 0.01 = 422.84

Step 3. Add it back up: 1,500.00 + 422.84 + 253.70 + 169.13 = 2,345.67
        That is the paycheck exactly.

Check: mom reserve 1,500.00 | savings 422.84 | needs 253.70 | wants 169.13


========================================================================
EXAMPLE 2. The mom reserve is close to its cap (rule 3.2)
========================================================================

Before payday: mom reserve $2,600.00. Everything else $0.00.

Paycheck                                         3,000.00

Step 1. The reserve takes $1,500.00 off the top, but only $400.00 fits
        under the $3,000.00 cap (3,000.00 - 2,600.00 = 400.00).
        To mom reserve                             400.00
        Does not fit, goes to savings            1,100.00
        Rest to split (3,000.00 - 1,500.00)      1,500.00

Step 2. Split the rest.
        Savings 50% of 1,500.00 =   750.00
        Needs   30% of 1,500.00 =   450.00
        Wants   20% of 1,500.00 =   300.00
        No rounding needed.

Step 3. Buckets after payday.
        Mom reserve  2,600.00 + 400.00            = 3,000.00 (at the cap)
        Savings      1,100.00 + 750.00            = 1,850.00
        Needs                                         450.00
        Wants                                         300.00
        Paycheck check: 400.00 + 1,850.00 + 450.00 + 300.00 = 3,000.00

Check: mom reserve 3,000.00 | savings 1,850.00 | needs 450.00 | wants 300.00


========================================================================
EXAMPLE 3. A paycheck smaller than the mom reserve (rule 3.2)
========================================================================

Before payday: mom reserve $2,000.00. Everything else $0.00.

Paycheck                                         1,200.00

Step 1. The reserve would take $1,500.00, but the paycheck is only
        $1,200.00, so it can take at most $1,200.00.
        Room under the cap: 3,000.00 - 2,000.00 = 1,000.00.
        To mom reserve                           1,000.00
        Does not fit, goes to savings              200.00
        Rest to split                                0.00

Step 2. Nothing is left to split. Needs and wants get $0.00.

Check: mom reserve 3,000.00 | savings 200.00 | needs 0.00 | wants 0.00


========================================================================
EXAMPLE 4. Payday with money left over and an overspent bucket
           (rules 3.2, 3.3, 3.4, 3.5, 3.8)
========================================================================

Before payday:
        Savings 0.00 | Needs 85.20 | Wants -12.50 (overspent) |
        Mom reserve 3,000.00 (at the cap)
        The savings account at the bank holds 3,000.00.
        Leftover setting: ask each time.

Paycheck                                         2,800.00

Step 1. Leftovers from the period that just ended.
        Needs has 85.20 unspent, so the app asks: roll over or move?
        Wants is at -12.50. It carries over as a negative. Nothing to ask.

Step 2. The reserve is at its cap, so the whole 1,500.00 goes to
        savings instead.
        Rest to split: 2,800.00 - 1,500.00 = 1,300.00
        Savings 50% = 650.00 | Needs 30% = 390.00 | Wants 20% = 260.00

Step 3. Buckets after payday, before you answer.
        Savings  1,500.00 + 650.00 = 2,150.00
        Needs       85.20 + 390.00 =   475.20
        Wants      -12.50 + 260.00 =   247.50
        Mom reserve                  3,000.00

Step 4. You answer "move to savings" for needs. The 85.20 moves.
        Needs    475.20 - 85.20 =   390.00
        Savings  2,150.00 + 85.20 = 2,235.20

Step 5. How much should be in the savings account (rule 3.8)?
        Savings + mom reserve = 2,235.20 + 3,000.00 = 5,235.20
        The account holds 3,000.00, so you still need to move 2,235.20.

Check: mom reserve 3,000.00 | savings 2,235.20 | needs 390.00 | wants 247.50 | still to move 2,235.20
