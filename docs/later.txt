FIXES FOUND ON CONNECTION DAY

1. FIX. Setup day and pending card payments (QUESTIONS.md B226).
   A card payment still pending is worded differently by the bank
   ("PAYMENT TO ACCT #nnnn ON mm/dd VIA WEB") from the posted one ("Mobile
   Banking payment to CRD nnnn Confirmation#"). No bank rule fit it, so it
   was read as a purchase and the starting money came out low by its
   amount. Fixed on the day with two bank rules. To build: setup day points
   out a pending item the bank labels as a card payment or transfer that is
   being read as a purchase, and offers a rule for its wording.

2. FIX. Suggestions for the arriving side of card payments (B227).
   The suggestions covered money leaving checking for each card, but not
   the same money arriving on the card ("PAYMENT FROM CHK nnnn"); without a
   rule it reads as a refund. Added by hand on the day. To build: suggest
   it from the history (the same amount the other way, a day or so later).

3. DECIDE. The "totals do not match" alert when Plaid lags (B230).
   Oct 13 morning: the everyday card's balance at the bank already showed
   the card payment, but Plaid's list of transactions still had it pending,
   so the self-check was off and the alert went out (rule 10.4 tells it at
   once). It clears by itself when Plaid sends the posted transactions.
   Options: keep as is; or tell it only once a gap has lasted a few syncs
   in a row (3, say), so timing gaps never reach the phone. A rulebook
   change (10.4), his call after a few days of seeing how often it happens.

4. FIX if it comes back. The split paycheck (B228).
   His employer sent part of each paycheck to savings and part to checking;
   the app knows one deposit per paycheck. He moved all of it to checking.
   If a split payday still comes: the checking part "This is my paycheck",
   the savings part "Other income". Only if he ever wants the split again:
   teach the app that two deposits the same day are one paycheck (option A
   of that day: the mom reserve once, then the split of the whole).
