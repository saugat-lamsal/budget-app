BOTTOM LINE

Phase 4's finish line is "an agent completes a full simulated pay period
through the screens and every number matches". It is crossed:

  Oct 16, through the real server's screens in Chromium and WebKit. Every

WHAT IS NOT TESTED, AND WHY

- Phone alerts (Phase 5). The simulator checks every alert every simulated
  day (section 10), but nothing is sent to a phone yet.
- Hosting, backups, and the iPhone itself (Phase 6). The screens are tested
  at the iPhone's size in Chromium and WebKit (Safari's engine), not on a
  phone. Passkeys are tested only in Chromium's virtual authenticator; WebKit
  has none, so its tests log in the tests' own way (login-for-tests.ts).
- The real bank (Phase 7). Everything uses the made-up bank in Plaid's
  format, and two recorded answers from Plaid's test bank.
- In this pay period: no card statement closes, so Home shows no card
  reminder in it (section 9 is tested elsewhere, and in the simulator every
  day); and no early paycheck (3.7), money from savings (4.9), old card
  purchase (5.4), payment marked "for mom" (7.6), or mom payment bigger than
  the reserve (8.3). Each has its own tests and is in the simulated lives.
- A32 and A33 are built as they are now until he answers.
