PR 1: THE BUG, THEN THE NEW LOOK

1. The bug he reported: "nothing shows on the home screen of the phone when
   things are low, no amount or nothing." Checked first (read-only on the
   live books, his OK). The report is in the PR.

2. The new look, through ui/tokens.css, base.css, blocks.css and blocks.tsx
   only (screens keep using the shared blocks):
   - Colors: background #050506 with a radial top glow to #020203; surfaces
     as soft white gradients (6% to 2%) with hairline borders (6 to 10%
     white); text #EDEDEF, muted #8A8F98; accent #5E6AD2 for fills (white on
     it passes AA). Accent as text uses a lighter shade (#8F98E8), since
     #5E6AD2 on the dark background is under 4.5:1. Status colors stay
     meaningful and come with a word or icon: amber low/warning, teal
     good/savings, red danger/mismatch.
   - Layered shadows on cards (border highlight, soft diffuse, ambient, and
     a 1px top edge highlight); accent glow on primary buttons.
   - Ambient background: indigo/violet blobs floating slowly (8 to 10 s), a
     faint 64px grid and faint noise, fixed behind the content, transform and
     opacity only, off with reduced motion.
   - Type: Geist for text, Geist Mono for small uppercase labels, self-hosted
     (@fontsource/geist and @fontsource/geist-mono, pinned, latin subset).
     Gradient on the hero number and screen titles. Tabular figures.
   - Tab bar: frosted glass, a small glowing accent line over the active tab.
   - Home: a hero card for safe to spend, then an uneven grid: Wants tall
     beside Needs and To sort.
   - The pay-period ring on the hero draws in with PR 2 (it needs the pay
     period's start from the server; the hero is built to take it).

Notes on how the gradients are done, so the contrast test can still read
them: axe cannot read a text color over a CSS gradient, so a card's gradient
is a pseudo-element behind its content (the card itself has a flat
translucent background) and gradient text is a mask on solid light text.


PR 2: MOTION
"motion" (Motion for React) and "@number-flow/react", pinned. Rolling digits
on safe to spend and bucket amounts; the pay-period ring draws in on Home;
screen change fade and rise; staggered card entrances; bottom sheets slide up
and can be dragged down to close; tab indicator and segmented controls slide;
the Home hero fades, scales and sinks slightly on scroll; a touch spotlight
follows the finger on the hero and bucket cards. Quick, precise: 200 to 300 ms,
expo-out cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1), entrances up to 600 ms, movements 4 to 8 px,
no bounce or spring overshoot.


PR 2, as built (Oct 18):
- "motion" 14.3.0 (pinned). @number-flow/react was tried and dropped: it draws
  in a shadow root and writes a <style> element, which the Content Security
  Policy (style-src 'self') blocks, so its digits were never clipped. The CSP is
  not loosened; the rolling digits are written in the app (blocks.tsx
  RollingDigits, blocks.css .roll): columns of 0 to 9 behind a window, moved by
  a CSS transition (300 ms, expo-out). The amount stays in the page as plain
  text under them (.money__text); the digits are drawn from data-d by CSS, so
  they are no text to read or copy.
- The server's Home view carries payday.periodStart (the day the book counted
  the last paycheck, or setup day in the first pay period); nothing in
  packages/money changed.
- Screen change and staggered entrances are CSS (.screen > * rise, 360 ms,
  6 px, 40 ms apart); the ring, the tab line, the hero on scroll and the sheet
  drag use Motion. The spotlight is a CSS variable set on pointer move.
- Sheets: a pull of more than 110 px, or more than 40 px faster than 0.6 px/ms,
  closes; anything less slides back.
